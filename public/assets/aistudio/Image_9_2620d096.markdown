## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an immersive, documentary-grade scientific environment tailored for secondary education (Grade 9) and astronomy enthusiasts. It balances theatrical cinematic wonder with precise, non-distracting pedagogical clarity.

### Personality & Emotional Tenor
- **Awe-Inspiring & Deep:** Visual depth echoes the quiet isolation and scale of deep space, framing scientific objects as hero artifacts.
- **Instrument-Grade Precision:** Interface elements evoke telescope telemetry, spectral readouts, and spaceflight navigation HUDs without overwhelming cognitive load.
- **Approachable Rigor:** Scientific data, physical units, and orbital mechanics are presented with high legibility, clean visual hierarchies, and contextual micro-explanations.

### Design Movement: Cinematic Glassmorphism & Sci-Fi HUD
The interface pairs deep dark-mode staging with multi-tiered translucent glass planes, razor-thin luminous borders, and directional optical flares. Interfaces float cleanly over 3D WebGL solar canvas viewports without obstructing planetary orbits.

## Colors

The palette is engineered exclusively for an immersive dark context, establishing an infinite cosmic backdrop punctured by luminous celestial accents.

### Palette Architecture
- **Cosmic Abyss (`#050812`):** Primary canvas ground. Acts as the deepest void behind interactive 3D WebGL viewports.
- **Stellar Dark Navy (`#0A1020`):** Secondary foundation for embedded trays, side sheets, and modal underlays.
- **Glass Panel Surface (`rgba(16, 26, 43, 0.72)`):** Neutral elevated plane with backdrop blur for astronomical HUDs and readout panels.
- **Primary Luminescence (`#65D9FF`):** Cyan photon highlight used for primary focal points, active orbital paths, telemetry indicators, and interactive states.
- **Secondary Ice (`#8AA9FF`):** Stellar blue used for atmospheric layers, secondary data metrics, sub-navigations, and passive badges.
- **Solar Corona (`#FFC875` with `#FFA726` delta):** Solar and radiation thermal indicators, cautionary boundaries, solar mass indicators, and quiz rewards.

### Text Contrast Tiers
- **Primary Readout (`#F4F7FB`):** High-clarity white-blue for titles, primary measurements, and body prose.
- **Secondary Telemetry (`#A6B2C5`):** Balanced slate for technical labels, metadata, and orbital stats.
- **Muted Grid (`#718096`):** Low-emphasis coordinates, grid lines, and passive timecodes.

## Typography

The typography supports dual-script parity: Persian-first (RTL default) and English (LTR).

### Dual-Script Typography Strategy
- **Persian Engine (Default):** Use `Vazirmatn` across all hierarchy levels. Persian body copy demands an extra `0.15` line-height ratio over Latin metrics to accommodate extended ascenders, descenders, and tashkeel marks.
- **Latin Engine:** Headings employ `Space Grotesk` for geometric authority, body copy uses `Sora` for rounded legibility, and technical instrumentation uses `Space Mono` for tabular readings.
- **Bidirectional Alignment:** In RTL mode, numbers and units (e.g., `AU`, `km/s`, `10²⁴ kg`) stay tabular and left-to-right oriented inside numerical spans while label descriptors align naturally to the right.
- **Scientific Monospace:** Astronomical coordinates, timecodes, orbital eccentricity, and telemetry tables strictly enforce tabular figures (`tnum`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored to a hybrid viewport system: an unrestricted underlying 3D WebGL canvas layered beneath an absolute 12-column responsive HUD grid.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with fixed overlay docking areas. Left/Right margins are `2.5rem`, columns separated by `1.5rem` gutters. HUD panels occupy 3 to 4 columns, keeping the central 5 to 6 columns open for planetary rendering.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Planetary metrics collapse into an anchored bottom drawer.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` canvas margins and `0.75rem` gutters. Side panels convert into swipeable bottom sheets with peek heights allowing 3D view interaction.

### HUD Density & Spatial Rules
- Primary telemetry panels maintain a fixed minimum distance of `space-xl` from screen edges on desktop to preserve optical framing.
- Content groupings inside telemetry cards follow an internal modular rhythm based on `space-xs` (tight metadata pairs) to `space-md` (metric blocks).

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial layering relies on glass refraction, tonal stratification, and targeted luminescent backlighting rather than standard drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Glass Layers
- **Surface Level 0 (Cosmic Floor):** Completely transparent surface showcasing dynamic 3D stellar environments.
- **Surface Level 1 (Floating HUD Panels):** `rgba(16, 26, 43, 0.65)` with `16px` backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), framed with a single-pixel perimeter stroke of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Interactive Drawers & Modals):** `rgba(16, 26, 43, 0.90)` with `24px` backdrop blur, framed with a boundary stroke of `rgba(101, 217, 255, 0.22)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Popovers & Tooltips):** `#0A1020` solid core with `rgba(101, 217, 255, 0.4)` accent halo.

### Luminescent Glow & Optical Halos
- Interactive buttons and active focus states use subtle photon halos: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(101, 217, 255, 0.25)`.
- Critical celestial markers (such as the Sun or volcanic moons) use a warm secondary halo: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(255, 200, 117, 0.3)`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework balances aerospace precision with organic planetary curvature.

### Geometry Rules
- Standard containers, cards, and modal sheets utilize moderate curvature (`0.5rem` / `roundedness: 2`) to maintain architectural structure.
- Navigation chips, orbital step nodes, and status indicators employ pill styling (`rounded-full`) to complement planetary circular aesthetics.
- HUD corners occasionally feature chamfered or angled accent trims (`45deg` micro-cuts on badge caps) to reinforce technical instrumentation.

## Components

### 1. Buttons & Controls
- **Primary Action (Hyper-Drive):** Background tinted with `rgba(101, 217, 255, 0.15)`, bordered with `1px solid #65D9FF`, text colored `#65D9FF`. On hover: background `#65D9FF`, text `#050812`, with an optical photon glow of `16px`.
- **Secondary Action (Telemetry):** Background `rgba(138, 169, 255, 0.08)`, border `rgba(138, 169, 255, 0.2)`, text `#F4F7FB`.
- **Icon Telemetry Buttons:** Circular `40px` glass targets with centered vectors, displaying cyan focus rings on engagement.

### 2. HUD Metric Badges
- Compact glass containers grouping an astronomical acronym (`AU`, `MASS`, `TEMP`) in `label-mono-sm` over bold quantitative values in `headline-sm`.
- Labeled with tiny directional status dots: Cyan for nominal planetary orbit, Amber for extreme surface hostility or retrograde motion.

### 3. Circular Planetary Progress Rail
- A persistent floating radial or horizontal step rail visualizing distance from the Sun.
- Nodes represent planetary bodies with circular proportional rings. Active planets trigger a pulsing cyan orbital aura. In RTL mode, the progression originates from the right (Sun) flowing toward the left (Kuiper belt).

### 4. Floating Orbital Navigation
- Floating bottom-center or top-nav glass bar with pill-shaped active state slides.
- Contains quick filters: Terrestrial, Gas Giants, Ice Giants, Dwarf Planets, Asteroids.

### 5. Interactive Planet Drawer
- Collapsible side dock (Right docked for LTR, Left docked for RTL default).
- Width: `420px` desktop, `100vw` bottom-sheet on mobile.
- Interior sections: 3D Core Cross-section toggle, Atmosphere Gas Breakdown (visualized as stacked horizontal emission lines), and Orbital Characteristics table.

### 6. Quiz Cards (Pedagogical Engine)
- Glass panels with high-contrast multiple-choice selectors.
- State responses: Correct answers trigger an immediate cyan bloom and `#65D9FF` border flash; incorrect answers emit a deep solar crimson pulse with corrective scientific notes.

### 7. Astronomical Comparison Matrix
- Tabular side-by-side comparison screen allowing users to benchmark two celestial bodies (e.g., Earth vs. Mars).
- Features dual split-view headers with synchronised metric meters and horizontal comparative range bars.
